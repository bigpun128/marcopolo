About Kitchen & Bath at the show

Planning a kitchen or bathroom project is one of the most popular reasons Southwest Florida homeowners visit the Fort Myers Home & Garden Show. The Kitchen & Bath category brings remodelers, cabinet makers, countertop fabricators, tile specialists and fixture showrooms together on one floor, so you can compare materials, gather quotes and meet the people who will actually do the work.

Whether you are refreshing a single powder room or gutting a whole kitchen, walking the show is the fastest way to see real samples in person, ask questions face to face, and leave with a shortlist of trusted local pros. Bring your measurements and a few photos of your space and the exhibitors below can point you in the right direction.

Tips for your visit

  • Bring rough measurements and photos of your current space.
  • Ask each exhibitor about lead times, since dry season books up fast.
  • Collect quotes from two or three vendors before you decide.

When is the best time to remodel a kitchen in SW Florida?

The cooler, drier winter months are ideal, which is exactly why the show runs in January. Many remodelers offer show specials for projects booked on-site.

Do exhibitors give free estimates?

Most Kitchen & Bath exhibitors offer free in-home consultations. Grab a card at their booth and schedule while you are at the show.
