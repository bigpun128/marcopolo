About Landscaping & Outdoor at the show

Outdoor living is a year-round way of life in Southwest Florida, and the Landscaping & Outdoor category at the Fort Myers Home & Garden Show is where homeowners plan it. You will find paver and hardscape crews, native-plant nurseries, outdoor-kitchen builders, and irrigation and lighting specialists all in one place.

Winter is the ideal season to plan and install outdoor projects here, before the summer heat and rains arrive. Walk the aisles to see materials in person, get ideas for your yard, and connect with the local crews who can bring the plan to life.

Tips for your visit

  • Sketch your yard or bring a photo so exhibitors can advise on layout.
  • Ask about Florida-friendly, low-water plantings.
  • Book installs early — dry season is peak season for outdoor work.

What outdoor projects are best to start in January?

Pavers, patios, outdoor kitchens and new plantings all do well when installed in the cool, dry winter months, so they are established before summer.

Are the plants at the show Florida-friendly?

Yes. Many nursery exhibitors focus on native and Florida-friendly species that thrive in our climate with less water and maintenance.
